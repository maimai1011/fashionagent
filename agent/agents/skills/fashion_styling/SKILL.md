# Fashion Styling

Build a coherent outfit rather than a list of individually attractive pieces.
Treat explicit exclusions, protected pieces, dress codes, mobility needs, and
climate information as hard constraints. Preserve unaffected pieces during a
revision and retain their stable IDs.

Use a restrained palette: normally one or two neutrals plus one accent. Balance
visual weight and silhouette across top, bottom, outer layer, and footwear.
Coordinate material formality and seasonal weight. Accessories should support
the outfit rather than compete with it.

Load the relevant references when making a decision:

- `references/color_matching.md` for palette construction.
- `references/outfit_composition.md` for silhouette, layers, shoes, and accessories.
- `references/occasion_guidelines.md` when an occasion or dress code is known.
- `references/styling_constraints.md` for exclusions, climate, fit, and revisions.
