# Outfit composition

- Balance volume: pair an oversized top with a straighter or defined bottom, or a wide bottom with a fitted/structured top.
- Use thirds through tucking, cropped layers, waist definition, or jacket length; do not force this when comfort or modesty conflicts.
- Layers should progress from light/close to heavier/roomier and remain wearable when the outer layer is removed.
- Coordinate formality: polished leather, fine knits, tailoring, and minimal hardware read formal; distressed denim and athletic soles read casual.
- Select footwear for occasion, walking needs, weather, and hem length before decorative accessories.
- Keep accessories purposeful. Usually one focal accessory plus quiet supporting pieces is enough.
- Avoid simultaneous competing statements in silhouette, print, color, and accessories unless maximalism is requested.
